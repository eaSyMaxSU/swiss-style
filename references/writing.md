# Writing

Structure is not enough. A presentation has a bar the template cannot check.

## Shared

- A display title is 1–3 Latin words or 2–6 Chinese characters. If the real title is longer, use `.display.m` or cut words. Do not set 30pt to make it fit.
- The first sentence carries the claim. Context follows.
- A number you cannot verify is a magnitude ("about 2,000") or `[DATA NEEDED: what]`. It is never a fake precise figure.
- Year and place are short: `2026`, `Zurich`, `Basel`.
- Do not pad. Two real projects stay two projects.
- Do not restate the heading in the first sentence.

## Slides

Read `deck-preflight.md`. The assertion is a sentence, not a topic label. Support is one line or up to four numbered points. If the slide looks full, it is too full.

The title slide counts as a slide in `content.slides`: its assertion is the short deck title. Each following entry is one assertion slide. `brief.page_target` is the total count, including the title slide. Build a sequence of claims, not a long document split across pages.

## Sources

For a company, product, release, price, or market figure, note the source and the date next to the fact or in `.meta`. Personal material the user wrote does not need a citation. Conflicting sources get one question, not a silent average.
