# Writing

Structure is not enough. Each type has a bar the template cannot check.

## Shared

- A display title is 1–3 Latin words or 2–6 Chinese characters. If the real title is longer, use `.display.m` or cut words. Do not set 30pt to make it fit.
- The first sentence carries the claim. Context follows.
- A number you cannot verify is a magnitude ("about 2,000") or `[DATA NEEDED: what]`. It is never a fake precise figure.
- Year and place are short: `2026`, `Zurich`, `Basel`.
- Do not pad. Two real projects stay two projects.
- Do not restate the heading in the first sentence.

## One-pager

The reader gets the point in half a minute. Three or four figures, one lead, two short sections, one closing line. 200–350 English words or 400–600 Chinese characters. The bottom of the page stays open.

## Long document

The cover is a poster: number, title, thesis, then empty paper or one geometric block. Chapters do not begin with "This chapter discusses". A source line is `.meta` under the paragraph that needs it. 6–15 pages. If a chapter is thin, merge it. Do not add a pull-quote card.

## Letter

One page. The recipient is the large line. Three short paragraphs in seven columns. The sign-off is flush left under the column. No motto, no seal, no centered valediction.

## Resume

Two pages when the career supports two pages. A bullet is a verb, the scope, and a result: "Cut invoice cycle from 11 days to 4 for a 40-person team." A bullet that only names a duty is not done. Dates and place sit in the right columns. Skills are words in the grid, not pills. Insert `.break` only after page 1 is actually full.

## Portfolio

Each case: number, short title, year, place, one photograph or one rectangle, one paragraph. Alternate which side holds the image. 3–6 pages. Do not invent a third image to balance a spread.

## Slides

Read `deck-preflight.md`. The assertion is a sentence, not a topic label. Support is one line or up to four numbered points. If the slide looks full, it is too full.

## Equity report

The thesis is one paragraph. Three figures. A table whose source is named. Two catalysts and two risks, each a fact plus why it matters. A missing quote stays `[DATA NEEDED: price]`.

## Changelog

The version is the large type. Notes are what changed, not why the team is excited. Dates are `2026.04.02` or an equally plain form.

## Landing page

The name is the poster. The lead is one or two sentences. Work and contact are the only sections unless the schema grows with the template. No pricing theatre, no logo cloud, no three identical cards.

## Sources

For a company, product, release, price, or market figure, note the source and the date next to the fact or in `.meta`. Personal material the user wrote does not need a citation. Conflicting sources get one question, not a silent average.
